Q / 01

Why hire you when I could just use AI myself?

Go for it. You might get further than you expect. I'm not the person you need, I'm the person you choose, the way you hand off the laundry knowing perfectly well how a washing machine works. Call me at the wall, where the question stops being how and becomes which.

Q / 02

What happens to my product if you disappear?

You own everything from day one and I set it up that way deliberately. Documented, tested, and handed off clean enough that anyone competent could continue it.

Q / 03

Faster than an agency and for a fraction of what one quotes, because there's no layer between you and the person building it. Real numbers come after the call, once I know what your idea actually requires. Quoting before that is guessing.
